## What the company is

Sound and lighting rental and production: professional sound and lighting hire, with the crew to run it. Self-description: "an independent creative production house bringing sound, light and space together. Technically serious, creatively ambitious and selective about the work we take on."

- **Events served:** club nights, concerts, festivals and open-air, corporate and brand events, private celebrations

## Story and voice

Started by two best friends, Sankarsh and Harsh, who loved going to shows and kept noticing what was missing: sound without depth, lights without intention. "It was never just about the sound. It was about the feeling."

Recurring ideas to write from:

- Sound is felt — depth, clarity, warmth, impact; "the kick in your chest"
- Every frequency, every beam, every transition should have a purpose
- Production is more than equipment: design, rigging, tuning, an operator until the last track
- "Built by music lovers. Driven by the experience."

Tone: calm, confident, plain sentences, no hype words, no exclamation marks. Short lines. Technical when it helps, never showing off.
